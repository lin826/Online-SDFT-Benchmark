# Dataset Quality Contract

[← Documentation](README.md)

The live synthetic generator is `semantic-title-body-sharp-t001`. Its catalog addresses
text/latent contradictions, route decisions that miss stated deadlines,
clipped probability masses, repeated content, and unrealistic placeholder
reuse. Each event's hidden preference is sampled from normalized utility
weights sharpened at temperature `0.01` instead of being assigned the utility
argmax deterministically.

## Dataset invariants

- Every category has explicit low, routine, and urgent scenarios with unique
  IDs and absolute latent-state centers.
- A rendered 10-minute deadline has a higher latent deadline mean than 15, 20,
  or 30 minutes.
- `LATER` cannot be the utility-optimal route when its 120-minute digest would
  arrive after an explicit useful horizon.
- Low-salience content cannot make `INTERRUPT` utility-optimal through a
  blanket category or regime bonus.
- On-call, manager-focus, and off-hours-social modifiers scale with scenario
  salience rather than applying to every item in a category.
- Importance, deadline, affinity, and busy state use logit-normal samples
  strictly inside `(0, 1)`, without clipping or endpoint point masses.
- Category counts differ by at most one per stream; local timestamps are
  ordered within each regime, including one midnight wrap for off-hours.
- Work projects, merchants, products, social interests, and promotional
  departments use separate vocabularies. Exact title/body pairs do not repeat
  within a stream. Content and stable entity preferences are seeded, so streams
  differ while remaining reproducible.
- Nonnegative evaluator utilities are normalized directly, so weights
  `(20, 30, 50)` first become base probabilities `(0.20, 0.30, 0.50)`. If an
  event has signed utilities, its negative minimum is subtracted before
  normalization. Sampling temperature `0.01` then produces
  $q_i\propto p_i^{100}$ in stable log space while preserving exact zero
  support. A dedicated seeded RNG samples one preference per event; the stored
  draw is reused for scoring, feedback, and every paired method.
- Accuracy is exact equality between the predicted action and that stored
  hidden sampled preference. Similar utility, tied utility, or the
  utility-optimal route does not substitute for the sampled action label. Only
  regret directly compares numeric evaluator utilities.

Tests enforce these constraints across multiple seeds. Canonical generation is
pinned to NumPy 2.4.6. A SHA-256 fingerprint over seeds 0–2 locks rendered model
inputs, latent state, evaluator utilities, preference distributions, and
sampled preferences:

```text
986cdf1a7d5fcc04c2b33f1bf90a1fc4f24a97ee85e663370382d8a67e4c932d
```

## Result provenance

Online-SDFT and REINFORCE were tuned directly on canonical seeds 0–2, with no
disjoint confirmation set. The REINFORCE screen required pooled exact matches
above Base and pooled total regret below Base, then ranked passing candidates
by accuracy first and regret second. RFT shares Online-SDFT's reset rank-4, alpha-8 LoRA
architecture and 172,032 trainable parameters, but its independently selected
student schedule uses replay size `32`, epsilon `0.06`, and learning rate
`7e-4`; its K=1 categorical proposal temperature is `8.0`. Those RFT
temperature/LR choices were screened on seed 1200. The tracked metrics and
figures were generated by the canonical six-method evaluation rather than
relabeled from exploratory runs.

The generator produces templated synthetic text; a real or de-identified
notification corpus would be a separate instrument.
